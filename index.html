Automated Outcome Tracking & Follow-Up System

Multi-channel cadence (30d, 90d, 180d, 365d, 2yr, 5yr) — SMS / WhatsApp / IVR / Assisted Field Verification

📞 Automated Government Follow-Up Engine

Scheduled multi-channel verification post-training completion
1. Digital Follow-up: Automated SMS and WhatsApp messages sent at 30 Days (Did you get employment?), 90 Days (Current job & wage), 180 Days (Still employed or not?), 365 Days, 2 Years & 5 Years.
2. IVR Call Assisted (Voice Response): For candidates who do not respond digitally, an automated IVR call asks: "Press 1 if Employed, Press 2 if Self-Employed, Press 3 if Unemployed".
3. Assisted Field Verification: If digital and IVR contact fails, District Skilling Officers perform field audit verification under government protocol.
